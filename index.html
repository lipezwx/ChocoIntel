    Warehouse positions · 17 November 2026

    Transfer plan

    3-day demand first, then safety stock, then the best dedicated strategic fill.

    3-day requirement, safety and strategic fill

    Kg per pallet = DC stock ÷ occupied dedicated positions (default 1,000 kg if none). Priority: 3-day demand → named safety stock → dedicated strategic fill. Two free daily transfers (morning and afternoon, 24 positions each); above 48 pallets a paid extra trip adds 28 positions. Sources: 3001 = North Hub, 3002 = East Hub. Batches are picked FEFO.

    Ready-to-go transfer

    Pallets are loaded by wave. The two daily trips are free; only pallets above 48 positions need a paid extra trip.

    Safety stock

    Named materials are topped up to their safety pallet target, even when 3-day demand is already covered. Target = 3-day demand + safety pallets, so the buffer remains after consumption.

    Rupture risk

    Materials that cannot be fully covered from the hubs. These need a source decision, not another pallet. Stock at other locations (e.g. 3009) is reported here, not silently mixed into the daily transfer.
